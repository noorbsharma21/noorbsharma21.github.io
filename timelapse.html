time:lapse

A plus A Gallery, San Marco, Venice
28 August to 25 October 2025

Covered by
Ville&Giardini art a part of cult(ure)

A group exhibition and public programme on how digital technologies and platform economies reshape our experience of time, space and connection. It treats simultaneity as a structural condition of late capitalism, where overlapping timelines produce fatigue, fragmentation and alienation.

It asks how practices of attentiveness might help us reimagine engagement, not as capture, but as care.

With work by Raqs Media Collective, Robert Blatt, Giovanni Borga, Camille Theodet, Roma Bantik and Kensuke Koike, and a screening programme with videos from the Servais Family Collection, Brussels. Exhibition views photographed by Clelia Cadamuro.
