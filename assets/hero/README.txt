Drop ONE photograph here. The first file is used as the hero.

It gets eroded into the white background automatically: the edges are
chewed away in blocks and speckles so the photo dissolves instead of
ending on a clean cut. Any jpg/png/webp works. Landscape or portrait.

Shoot or choose something with depth and a lot of green or sky, since
the dissolve reads best when the subject is dense in the middle and
loose at the edges.
