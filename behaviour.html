Centre for Social and Behaviour Change

Spring 2024. Research Assistant, for the Railway Ministry of India and the Gates Foundation. The brief: reduce smoking among passengers on the Intercity Express 14315.

  1. “Why Not to Smoke”A two-frame sign: a passenger lighting a cigarette, then the train on fire. It turns a prohibition into cause and effect, and gives a reason instead of a rule.
  2. The Ripple EffectThe fire reaches the smoker’s family. Smokers are numb to their own health risk; harm to the people they love lands harder.
  3. The Casual SparkA comic in which one discarded cigarette stub becomes a fire. Made for train toilets, where people have time to read a story through.
