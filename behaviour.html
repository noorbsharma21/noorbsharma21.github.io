Centre for Social and Behaviour Change

Spring 2023. Research Assistant at the Centre for Social and Behaviour Change. Client: Northern Railways. The collaterals were designed with the CSBC team.

The problem

Passengers smoking on the Intercity Express 14315, mostly in the toilets, where nobody is watching.

What the field visits found

A fire was not on smokers’ minds, and nobody expected to be caught.

Route 1

Make the risk of fire feel real

Route 2

Make the risk of getting caught feel real

The pilot

Ten days on the train: two of baseline, six with the collaterals up, two of endline. Four sets of coaches: none, fire, getting caught, both. 400 passengers, 100 to a set.

Measured by

A passenger survey, and the cigarette butts collected from each coach.

Flourish Science

Fall 2025 to Spring 2026. Strategy and Design Lead on Penn’s capstone in consulting for behavioural science, with Ivy Guo, Mavis Chen, Luna Su and Taylor Kirk. Client: Flourish Science, a wellness app with a companion called Sunnie.

My part: the first client deck, the competitive analysis and the team’s posters.

  1. Nov 2025Fifteen appsAudited 15 mental health apps: features, pricing, onboarding, positioning.
  2. Dec to JanAlignmentTwo counsellor interviews. Flourish chose to lead with journaling, safety planning and habit tracking.
  3. Feb to MarRecruitingPenn students in therapy or on waitlists, found through posters and QR codes in 8+ buildings.
  4. Mar to AprInterviews14 interviews and 3 follow-ups. Emotions are hard to name, and opening the app is a burden.
  5. Apr 2026SynthesisA prototype toolkit, built with NotebookLM, Claude and Figma.
The insight

The gap is between sessions. Before one, people need to surface what happened and what to ask; after it, somewhere to put what spilled over, and the next steps.

The Therapy Prep Toolkit

Seven steps around a single session, from setting it up to folding what it found into Flourish’s insights.

83% of the people we interviewed wanted to keep using Flourish. The toolkit added 6 new screens and 20 improvements to existing ones, across 7 new user flows.
