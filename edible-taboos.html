Edible Taboos

Ashoka University, Spring 2024. Curator.

An exhibition on abject art and what the body does with food. It applies Julia Kristeva’s abjection to eating: the revulsion certain foods and certain ways of eating provoke, which is never only about the food and always about the identity and the social order underneath it.

Its inspiration lies in Han Kang’s The Vegetarian.

The room was built as the argument. A clay-walled corridor shaped like a gut, with one entry and one exit, so a visitor walks the three stages in sequence and cannot take them in any other order.
